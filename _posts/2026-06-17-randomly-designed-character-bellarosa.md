I designed a character; the process was rather casual. I didn't think too much initially, I was just doodling. In the end, I ended up drawing a femboy character.

The settings in the top-left corner were added after I'd finished the drawing. During the colouring process, I was quite casual in my choice of colours, even intentionally making them look quirky.

The final result is rather good. You can also make out the male bone structure beneath the clothing, although it's not noticeable unless you look closely.

![drawing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zihong-xie/blog-image-hosting/main/img/2026-06-17-Bellarosa-von-Hamburg.png)

Bellarosa von Hamburg is just a pseudonym I made up by randomly picking names on Google Maps. 'Bellarosa' is an Italian name meaning 'beautiful rose', while 'von Hamburg' is German for 'from Hamburg'. It's basically a confusing name.

If I had to describe this character in three words, they would be: free-spirited, playful and mischievous.
