### B (2)
I've had another look and I think I've got your point now. It's true that English, especially online, often focuses too much on tone and has an anti-intellectual tendency.

I've come across similar issues on Reddit before; many people are too lazy to think and only care about your tone and wording. When I encounter such people, I have to admit that there really are those who lack critical thinking.

However, to be frank, this actually has nothing to do with the English language itself. It is the people who use it who are the real problem, not the language itself. If we are talking about English grammar and linguistic characteristics, it does indeed have its issues, but it certainly cannot be described as "the worst". Linguistically speaking, no language is superior to any other; whether it be English, French, Latin, Ancient Greek, Chinese, Japanese or any other, they are all capable of expressing the same ideas and logic, though in different ways. 

### B (3)
By the way, you might think it is English itself that is limiting your expression, or that others simply cannot understand what you mean, but frankly, it is also because you cannot communicate properly.

With all due respect, your text is a chaotic mix of fragmented arguments without explanations or examples, and examples or explanations without arguments, from your very first sentence, "If you know a lot about language you know English is one of the worst ways to communicate ong [sic]". Even translating it into ancient Greek or Latin would not make it any better.

I'm not talking sh*t. In other comments, I've seen that you are interested in Ancient Greek and Ancient Greek culture; and since Ancient Greek, Aristotle has already clearly presented the argument-evidence structure in his work *Rhetoric*.

You might think that the reason I couldn't understand you at first was due to the English language and English culture, but in fact, you've been using such fragmented expressions the whole time, leaving people only with a vague understanding. Do you really think that Latin and Ancient Greek alone can magically make up an entire line of reasoning for you?

Finally, if I may be so bold, the fact that I asked you those questions does not prove that "English is a poor language and that's why others can't understand me"; you've simply been evading the fundamentals of communication.

### B (4)
Furthermore, forgive me for needing to read your words multiple times to fully understand your point. You also said, "You cannot avoid being verbose or avoid being perceived as condescending if you want to communicate effectively", as a criticism of English; but in fact, don’t you think this applies to every language? As mentioned earlier, simply using other "profound languages" such as Latin or Ancient Greek will not make up an entire line of reasoning or something like that. Do you really think that elites such as Caesar and Cicero did not use more words back then in order to express themselves effectively? Do you think that the precise Latin grammar was sufficient on its own to allow them to say several sentences less? Meanwhile, looking back through history, whether it be Cicero’s orations, the writings of philosophers from various countries, or even the highly condensed classical Chinese texts, we can all find what might be perceived as a "condescending" tone in them. Basically, whenever you try to express your thoughts with precision, it will, more or less, come across as "condescending" in any language, whether you mean it to or not.

It could be said that your criticism of English applies to all languages; it is simply that different cultures and communities interpret these texts differently. In short, this remains a cultural phenomenon. From start to finish, you have merely been criticising the anti-intellectual tendencies of English internet culture, yet you have mistakenly directed this criticism towards the English language itself.
