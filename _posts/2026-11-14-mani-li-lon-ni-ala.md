---
title: "mani li lon ni ala. jan Wan li lanpan ala."
categories: [TokiPona, tok-literature]
tags: [literature]
lang: tok
description: "Chinese Proverb in Toki Pona"
---
"此地无银三百两，隔壁王二不曾偷" li toki lili pi sona suli lon ma Sonko. ona li toki sona e ni:

jan San li jo e mani mute.

ona li wile ala e ni "jan ante li sona li lanpan e mani ona" li len e mani ona lon anpa ma, taso ona li pilin e ni "jan ante li awen ken sona li awen ken lanpan" li sitelen lon lipu wan e ni: mani li lon ni ala.

jan Wan li lon tomo poka pi tomo ona li lukin li sona e mani ona.

jan Wan li lanpan e mani, taso ona li wile ala e ni "jan ante li sona e ni: ona li jan lanpan ike" li sitelen lon lipu ante e ni: jan Wan li lanpan ala.
