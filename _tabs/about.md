# Textbooks
## lawa toki pi toki pona
*lawa toki pi toki pona (The Grammar of Toki Pona)* is a Toki Pona grammar textbook written in August 2026, using just Toki Pona itself, which is a minimalist language, with just over a hundred words.

This demonstrates that Toki Pona is a language with a full capacity for expression.

- [lawa toki pi toki pona](/posts/lawa-toki-pi-toki-pona/)

## The Basic Grammar of Ido
*Basic Grammar of Ido* was written in April 2026, explaining the grammar of the constructed language Ido. It is available in three languages: Chinese, French and English.

- [伊多语基本语法](https://zh.wikibooks.org/wiki/%E4%BC%8A%E5%A4%9A%E8%AF%AD%E5%9F%BA%E6%9C%AC%E8%AF%AD%E6%B3%95)
- [La grammaire fondamentale de l'ido](https://fr.wikibooks.org/wiki/La_grammaire_fondamentale_de_l%27ido)
- [The Basic Grammar of Ido](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/The_Basic_Grammar_of_Ido)

# Translations
## sitelen suli pi ken jan

A translation of *the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen* in Toki Pona, written in August 2026. It demonstrates that a minimalist language can still convey profound thoughts.

- [sitelen suli pi ken jan](/posts/sitelen-suli-pi-ken-jan/)
